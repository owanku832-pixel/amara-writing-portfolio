Sample 01 · Contemporary Romance

The Last Table

The café was almost empty when Daniel arrived. He paused beside the table by the window, the one Maya had chosen every Thursday for three months.

"You're late," she said, keeping her eyes on her book.

"I know." He placed a small paper bag between them. "I brought an apology. It's chocolate cake, so I'm hoping you'll consider hearing me out."

Maya finally looked up. She wanted to stay annoyed. The problem was that he remembered everything, even the little things she pretended not to care about.

Sample 02 · Supernatural Mystery

The House That Remembered

At midnight, the old house began whispering again.

Nia stood at the foot of the staircase, listening. The voice was faint, almost lost beneath the rain, but it spoke her name with the familiarity of someone who had known her all her life.

She had been told never to open the locked room. She had also been told that her grandmother had never owned this house.

Tonight, both stories could not be true.

Sample 03 · Interactive Storytelling

A Choice at the Door

The elevator doors slide open. Across the lobby, Adrian is waiting beside the glass entrance.

"I didn't think you'd come," he says.

You can walk past him and protect your pride, or stop and ask why he called you here. Either way, tonight will change what happens next.
